How the whole thing works

A sender texts a photo to Mailroom's iMessage number. A short conversation collects a recipient and a note, then hands the card to Lob, which prints & mails a real postcard via USPS. First card free; then ~$1 (credits via buy → Stripe). Recipients write back by texting a REPLY code or — if the sender had no address — by tapping a forwarded claim link. "Pen pal" mode mails an anonymous stranger who writes back. A companion iOS app + App Clip show cards traveling and claim links natively.

States. idle → (photo) → awaiting_send_type → awaiting_recipient_name → awaiting_recipient_address → awaiting_address_confirm → awaiting_message → awaiting_send_confirm → sending → [celebration] → (first-timers) awaiting_sender_location → idle. Note the return-address step now lives after the send. Pen-pal & reply sends still collect the address up front (the match needs it). Off-script questions get a GPT-4o-mini reply in the bot's voice.
