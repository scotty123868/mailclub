How the whole thing works

A sender texts a photo to Mailroom's iMessage number. A bot walks them through one short conversation — recipient, address, a note — then hands the card to Lob, which prints and mails a real paper postcard via USPS. The first card is free; after that it's ~$1 (credits topped up with buy via Stripe). Recipients can write back by scanning a QR / texting a REPLY code, or — if the sender didn't have an address — by tapping a claim link and adding their own. There's also a "pen pal" mode that mails to an anonymous stranger who can write back. The companion iOS app + App Clip let people view cards travel on a map and claim links natively.

Entry → states → exit. Webhook authenticates & dedupes → checks global commands (cancel, photo-swap, REPLY, memories, buy) → routes on sms_conversation_state.step through: idle → (photo) → awaiting_send_type → awaiting_recipient_name → awaiting_recipient_address → awaiting_address_confirm → awaiting_message → awaiting_sender_location → awaiting_sender_address_confirm → awaiting_send_confirm → sending → [celebration] → idle. Off-script questions are answered by a GPT-4o-mini assistant in the bot's voice.

Top review notes — across the whole flow

What's genuinely great — protect it

  • Instant feedback before slow work (❤️ + "Got it" before upload).
  • The stamping celebration — postmark + station + "Off to Sarah" + inline photo/route. Signature moment.
  • Honest, recoverable errors that always name the next action and refund credits.
  • Reciprocation loop — proactively pinging the original sender ("a reply is on its way") is the retention engine.
  • Recipient memory + AI note ideas — texture greeting cards can't match.
  • Fail-closed moderation for stranger mail, explained kindly.

Highest-leverage improvements

  • 1. Unify the brand domain. app.themailroom.club vs themailroom.club vs mailroomclub.io appear across claim links, copy, and the support email. Mixed domains kill trust at payment/claim. Pick one.
  • 2. Deploy the real AASA at app.themailroom.club/.well-known/ — without it the App Clip + claim Universal Links silently fail. Make-or-break.
  • 3. Shorten time-to-first-send. Defer the full sender address (collect city/state now, full address only when a reply is actually needed). It's the longest pole before the magic moment.
  • 4. Add explicit next-step CTAs & magic words. End the welcome with "Send me a photo to begin"; hint "say send" / "say ideas" at those steps.
  • 5. Notify on unclaimed links before they expire (sender currently hears nothing).
  • 6. Defer the "penpal" fork until after a first successful friend send, so first-timers aren't asked to choose something they don't understand.

  4. Confirm the postcard-renders storage bucket exists; deploy all edge functions.
