Mailroom v0.7 · final design · sign-off

The signup flow
IS the first send.

One radical simplification reorganizes the whole product. The user cannot enter Mailroom until they have mailed a card. The "form" we ask them to fill out IS a postcard. Every field we need (their name, their address, the recipient's details) gets collected because they're literally addressing one. No skip, no preview, no browse first. They open the app, they mail a memory, they're in.

Time to First Send goes from 8–12 minutes (v0.6.x) to ~3 minutes. The user gets value before they get UI.

1

Signup is the first send

No skip button. No "complete profile later." The first thing the user does in Mailroom is mail a real postcard. Then they're in.

2

Two whys, one app

Primary: Mail a shared memory. Secondary: Send a stranger something nice. The pen pal feature is the second why — equally first-class.

3

Empty isn't allowed

The user has at least one postcard in their journal from the moment they finish onboarding. The empty-state of the app doesn't exist for new users.

4

Address gaps don't block

If they don't know the friend's address, send-a-link. The card queues and ships the moment the friend fills in the form. From the sender's view, it's sent.

Visual fix

The new credits pill: envelope + count.

The 1¢ stamp tile next to the count showed two numbers (a tiny "1¢" and a regular count). At a glance, looked like 13, 25, etc. Replaced with a clean envelope icon. One number, one meaning. Reads as "3 cards in your pocket" instantly.

Why this is the right move

Every form field we collect is one we'd collect anyway for the send. Every screen the user passes through is one they'd eventually visit. We didn't add work, we removed the option to defer it. The user gets to the value (a postcard in their journal, a real card heading to a real mailbox) in the same time it takes them to fill in a signup form today.

And the address-link option is the unlock that makes this never fail. If the user doesn't have a friend's address — or any friend at all — they can still send "send them a link," and that counts as their first send. The app opens. Their journal has a queued card. The recipient might fill in the address later, or not. Either way, the user is in.

After the first send

Five destinations. One button. One bar.

Tab order, left to right: Map · Constellation · [Send FAB] · Friends · My Card. Same five icons the existing app already uses (Map, Sparkles, Send, Users, User from Lucide). Send sits centered, raised, never moves. The Postcards journal you saw in the v0.6.x gallery is gone as its own tab — it lives inside My Card now, which is where you'd expect your own postcard history to be.

Magical moments · v0.7 build list

Five small things that make this feel alive.

Per your request, these are in the v0.7 to-do. None of them are screens. They're micro-moments that pay off the brand and reward the act of sending. Cheap to build, disproportionately sticky.

✉  The fold

On tap-Mail-it: the photo + note compose into a postcard, then a paper-fold animation folds the card into an envelope. ~700ms. Replaces the current "Sent!" alert. Fires at the end of signup AND every subsequent send.

Lib: react-native-reanimated 3 keyframes · Est: 2-3 hrs
📍  The pin drops

When the mail service confirms a card is in transit, a soft animation fires on the Map tab: red dot fades in at the destination city, pulses once. Solidifies on delivery. User checks the map and sees new dots over time.

Lib: mail-service webhook → Supabase fn → push → MapView callout · Est: 3-4 hrs
🌟  The reciprocation badge

When someone you mailed first mails YOU back, their card lands in your My Card journal with a soft gold ring + "Your pen pal" stamp overlay. The loop closing is the dopamine. Fires in Constellation too — their node gets the gold ring.

Lib: CSS state on inbound cards where outbound exists to same recipient · Est: 2 hrs
💬  The empty-week whisper

If you go a week without sending, the trailing tile in this week's row morphs from a quiet dashed "+" into a soft black tile that reads "Mail someone →". No push notification, no nag. Just a nudge inside the journal you'll already check.

Lib: conditional render on last-send-timestamp · Est: 1 hr
📣  The thunk

Physical postal "thunk" haptic + a soft mailbox-lid wav clip when the user taps Mail it. Tiny detail. Memorable disproportionately to build cost. Pair with the fold animation.

Lib: expo-haptics + expo-av · Est: 30 min

Total build cost for all five: ~9 hours. Two focused sessions with CC.

Constellation · not rebuilding, porting

Reuse the tête constellation work.

Short answer: we don't rebuild Constellation from scratch. Mailroom's current radial constellation is the simple version; the work you already did in teteapp-main is the production-grade version. We port it across, swap the data model from moments to postcards, and ship.

What teteapp's constellation already does

🌟  Force-directed social graph

d3-force simulation. Self fixed at center via fx/fy. Other nodes gravitate by link-distance + many-body repulsion. 120 ticks settled offline so it doesn't run during render.

🔗  Friend-of-friend edges

Public postcards between two of your friends surface as faint edges. The web grows with the NETWORK, not just you. Toggle on/off, default off.

🔍  Pan + pinch zoom

Zoom up to 3x to read dense clusters. Zoom out to 0.6x to fit 50+ friend graphs. Double-tap resets. Uses react-native-gesture-handler.

📋  Bottom-sheet drilldowns

Tap an edge → list of postcards exchanged on that line. Tap a node → profile overlay with avatar, shared count, swipeable gallery of cards. Tap your own node → clears.

🔒  Privacy-aware

FoF only surfaces postcards visible to current user via the public-feed rule. No private mail leaks. Already vetted in tête.

✨  Reciprocation badge integration

The "gold ring" magical moment from the to-do above hooks straight into this graph — when a node reciprocates, its star glows gold. Same architecture, postcard-flavored.

The port, concretely

  1. Copy src/lib/constellationGraph.ts from teteapp → Mailroom src/lib/constellationGraph.ts.
  2. Rename the data model: LocalMoment → Postcard, participants → (sender_id, recipient_id), shared → delivered.
  3. Copy src/screens/ConstellationScreen.tsx → Mailroom app/(tabs)/constellation.tsx. Swap LocalMoment imports for the postcards API.
  4. Recolor: Mailroom's ink + paper palette in place of tête's velvet. Same dark sky for the constellation background. Keep the gold reciprocation accent.
  5. Wire the bottom-sheet drilldown to open postcard details (the existing route the journal already uses).
  6. Hook the reciprocation badge: any node where outbound + inbound both exist gets a gold ring. Same logic as the journal's gold-card overlay.

Estimated port: ~6–8 hours. Half the time a from-scratch build would take. The teteapp version is genuinely the better visualization — force-directed beats radial-spiral every time once the graph has more than ~8 people.

Concrete v0.7 build order

Phase by phase.

Before the redesign ships, the existing payment + mail-service wiring needs to actually work. None of this is magical if "Mail it" doesn't actually mail.

Phase A · foundations

  1. Wire payment + mail-service test keys. Real card goes from photo to USPS. No design changes yet. ~1 day
  2. Deploy the Phase 6.1 hardening migration + edge functions. Re-test send_postcard end-to-end on the new schema. ~2 hrs

Phase B · the keystone

  1. Build the forced signup → send flow. Replaces WelcomeSheet entirely. 7 screens: sign in → pick photo → write note → pick recipient → their info → your info → mailed. ~6-8 hrs
  2. Build the recipient picker with all four options (friend, send-link, self, pen pal). ~2 hrs
  3. Wire pen pal matching backend (random within tier — city/state/anywhere). Even a placeholder service works for v0.7. ~4-6 hrs

Phase C · the app on the other side

  1. Restructure the 5 tabs: Map · Constellation · [Send FAB] · Friends · My Card. Send becomes a centered raised FAB. Postcards is no longer a tab. ~2 hrs
  2. Redesign My Card: remove "Postcard Friends Since" line, swap the About-me grid for one editable bio textarea, trim stats to Friends · Sent · Received, attach the week-by-week journal beneath the stats. ~3-4 hrs
  3. Build the Map interaction: tap pin → animate connecting line to reciprocal pin → bottom-sheet postcard preview slides up. ~4 hrs
  4. Port teteapp constellation — copy constellationGraph.ts + ConstellationScreen.tsx, swap moments → postcards, restyle for Mailroom palette, full-bleed dark sky. ~6–8 hrs
  5. Clean up Friends: rename copy on the pen-pal CTA to "Send one, get one from a stranger." ~30 min
  6. Swap credits pill from 1¢-tile to envelope-icon + count. ~30 min

Phase D · magical moments

  1. The fold — postcard folds into envelope after Mail it. ~3 hrs
  2. The pin drops — mail-service webhook → map animation. ~3-4 hrs
  3. The reciprocation badge — gold ring on inbound cards from people you mailed first. ~2 hrs
  4. The empty-week whisper — trailing tile morphs into "Mail someone →" after a quiet week. ~1 hr
  5. The thunk — mailbox haptic + wav on send. ~30 min

Estimated total: ~42–54 hours with CC + you. Realistically 5–7 working sessions. Payment + mail-service wiring comes first; the keystone signup→send comes second; the constellation port and magical moments come last. Everything in Phase C/D is composable and can ship behind a flag if you want progressive releases.
