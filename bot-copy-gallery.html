What changed this round

  • Faster. The "who's this for?" question now lands instantly — the photo uploads in the background. The "Postmarked" celebration fires in ~2-3s; the animated card + route map follow a few seconds later.
  • No address? No problem. New "send a link" branch — your friend adds their own address.
  • Ask anything. Off-script messages get a real answer now, not a canned re-prompt.
  • Plain talk. Errors say what actually happened. No "flew the coop." No all-caps.
  • Less repetition. The reply flow no longer says "writing Sarah back" three times.

Notes

Natural replies work, any case: send it, yep, looks good, never mind, skip, buy 10, send a link.

Friend cards can be scheduled ("June 15", "in 3 days"). Pen pal cards ride the next Sunday drop. Replies mail right away.

AI is used only on misses (off-script questions) + the optional "ideas" note helper — a fraction of a cent. The whole happy path is instant and free.
