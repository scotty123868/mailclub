Common questions

How do credits work?

  • One credit equals one US dollar.
  • A handwritten note costs 1 credit. A photo postcard or a "place" card costs 2 credits. A custom card costs 5 credits.
  • Every new account gets 5 free credits.
  • You can buy more in the app under Settings → Credits, or directly from My Card.

How long does delivery take?

Once we're live with our printing partner: domestic US delivery is typically 5–9 business days from when you tap Send. International varies. During TestFlight beta, mail does not go to the post office yet; cards are saved to your drafts and you can preview them, but no physical mail is sent.

I added a friend's address and can't send to them.

Make sure the address has a real street, city, state, and ZIP. We require all four. If the address looks right but Send is greyed out, you may be out of credits — check your balance on My Card.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, tap "Forgot password?". We'll email a reset link to the address on your account.

I want to delete my account.

Settings → Delete account, then confirm twice. This permanently removes your profile, friends list, sent-mail history, uploaded photos, and credit balance. Credits are non-refundable.

The app crashed.

Please email support with: what you were doing, your iPhone model, and your iOS version. Screenshots help.

A photo I uploaded looks wrong on the postcard preview.

Photos are cropped to fit a standard 4×6 postcard back. Best results come from photos shot horizontally. If something looks off, please send us the photo + a screenshot of the preview.

I want a refund on a credit pack.

Refunds for in-app purchases are handled directly by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and follow the instructions. We can't process refunds on our end.

Status

If the app is acting up and you suspect it's a Mailroom outage rather than your phone, email us. We don't currently have a public status page — for a beta this small, it'd be theater.

TestFlight beta tester? Use the in-TestFlight feedback flow (shake your phone in the app, or tap "Send Beta Feedback" inside TestFlight). That feedback goes straight to us with the screenshot and device metadata pre-filled, which makes triage way faster.
