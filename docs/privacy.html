Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 11, 2026

Mailroom is a small iOS app for sending real, physical postcards. We try to collect only what we need to make the app work and to ship mail to the right place. This document explains what data we collect, what we do with it, and what your rights are.

What we collect

Account information

  • Email address — for sign-in, password recovery, and to send you delivery notifications you've opted into.
  • Display name — what shows up on the Mail Card your friends see. Provided by you.
  • City and state (optional) — appears on your Mail Card as your return city. You can leave this blank.

Mailing-related information

  • Mailing addresses you enter for friends — used solely to print and ship physical postcards to those friends.
  • Card contents — the message text, photos, and design choices you make. We store these so they can be reproduced on the printed card and so you can see your sent-mail history.
  • Photos you upload — stored in a private bucket scoped to your account. Other users cannot see them.

Payment information

  • Credit balance — we track how many credits you've purchased and spent. We do not store credit card numbers; in-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple's StoreKit and we never see your payment details.

Diagnostic information

  • Crash reports and basic usage analytics — anonymous information about app crashes so we can fix bugs. No personal identifiers are attached.

What we do NOT collect

  • Your contacts list, address book, calendar, or phone messages.
  • Your location (the app does not request location permission).
  • Health, financial, or browsing-history data.
  • Identifiers used for advertising or cross-app tracking.
No tracking, no ads. Mailroom does not use third-party advertising SDKs and does not share data with advertisers or data brokers.

How your data is used

  • To run the app: sign you in, show your friends, store your sent mail, deliver push notifications you've opted into.
  • To print and mail postcards: we share the recipient's mailing address and your card's content with our printing partner (Lob), solely so they can physically print and mail the card.
  • To improve the app: we look at aggregate, anonymous usage and crash data.

Who we share your data with

  • Supabase — hosts our database, authentication, and file storage. They process data on our behalf under their own privacy policy.
  • Lob — our printing and mailing partner. They receive only the data needed to print and mail a specific postcard.
  • Apple — handles in-app purchases. We receive a receipt and the credit pack you bought; Apple keeps your payment details.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone, ever.

Data retention

  • Account data is kept as long as your account is active.
  • Sent-mail records are kept so you can see your history.
  • If you delete your account in the app (Settings → Delete account), we permanently remove your profile, friends list, sent-mail records, photos, and credit history. This action cannot be undone.

Your rights

  • Access: email us and we'll send you a copy of your data.
  • Correction: you can edit your name, tagline, city, and friends directly in the app.
  • Deletion: use Settings → Delete account, or email us and we'll do it for you.
  • Portability: on request, we'll export your data in a standard format.

If you live in a region with specific privacy laws (GDPR, CCPA, etc.), you have additional rights under those laws. Email us to exercise any of them.

Children

Mailroom is rated 17+ on the App Store and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If you believe we have, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top and, where possible, notify you in-app. Continuing to use Mailroom after a change means you accept the updated policy.
