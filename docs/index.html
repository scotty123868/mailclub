A private postcard club.

Real handwritten notes and photo postcards, sent by you, to the people who matter.

Mailroom is a small iOS app that helps you send real, physical mail to your friends. Pick a category (a handwritten note, a photo postcard, a postcard about a place, or a custom card), write your message, and we print it and put it in the actual mail.

You start with five free credits. enough for two photo postcards or five notes. After that, you can buy credit packs in the app.

In beta. Mailroom is currently invitation-only on TestFlight. Public App Store launch is coming.

How it works

  • Make a Mail Card. your name, a tagline, where you're writing from.
  • Add friends by scanning their QR code in person, or by entering their mailing address.
  • Pick a card category, write, send. We print and mail.
  • Your friend can reply right on the card. that response makes its way back to you.

For TestFlight beta testers
